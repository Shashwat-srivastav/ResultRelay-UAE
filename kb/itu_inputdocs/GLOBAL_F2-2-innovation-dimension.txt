AI Ready – Analysis Towards a Standardized Readiness Framework, Report 2.0 — 2 / Innovation Ecosystem dimension (p.15)

The Innovation Ecosystem assesses the broader environment that nurtures and accelerates the advancement and adoption of Artificial Intelligence. It focuses on the critical inputs and collaborative dynamics that transform research into tangible progress and practical applications. We examine six key dimensions: standards, Engagement in Open source, R&D, investment, GenAI Content, and AI Technology market size.
