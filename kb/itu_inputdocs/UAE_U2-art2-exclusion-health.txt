Federal Decree-Law No. (45) of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data (PDPL) - unofficial English translation — Article 2 (p.3)

The provisions of this Decree Law shall not apply to the following: a. government data. b. government authorities that control or process Personal Data. c. Personal Data held with security and judicial authorities. d. a Data Subject who processes his/her data for personal purposes. e. health personal data that is subject to legislation regulating the protection and Processing thereof.
